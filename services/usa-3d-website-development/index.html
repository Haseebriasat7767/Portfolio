Frequently asked questions

What does a 3D website project include?

Scope depends on the brief, but a typical engagement can include creative direction, UX/UI, 3D/WebGL development, responsive implementation, performance QA, and launch support.

Can you work with a US team remotely?

Yes. Aether is set up for remote collaboration with product, brand, and real-estate teams across the United States and worldwide.

Does every project need WebGL?

No. The right solution might be live WebGL, a lighter 3D treatment, or a progressive enhancement around a fast HTML experience. The technology follows the story.
